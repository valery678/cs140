About Clove

Clove is a Controller agent from Scotland in VALORANT. Clove can use smokes even after dying, which makes this agent different from other Controllers.

Clove's Abilities and Features

Clove's abilities and gameplay features
Ability / Feature Key Type Description
Pick-Me-Up C Basic Gives temporary health and speed after a takedown.
Meddle Q Basic Weakens enemies with decay.
Ruse E Signature Creates smoke clouds to block vision.
Not Dead Yet X Ultimate Allows Clove to revive after dying.
After-Death Smoke E Special Feature Allows Clove to place smokes after death.
Clove is a Controller who can help the team even after dying.

Tips for Playing Clove

  • Use smokes to block enemy vision.
  • Use Meddle to weaken enemies.
  • Help your team enter sites.
  • Remember to use smokes after dying.
